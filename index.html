Beyond Bookkeeping

African retail does not fail from a lack of bookkeeping. It fails from shrinkage, uncollected debt, and capital invisibility.

Calling Waasz AI a "bookkeeping app" misses the point entirely. Traditional accounting software fails because it expects shop owners in bustling open markets to become desk accountants—opening heavy apps, logging into web dashboards, and filling out endless double-entry forms while managing suppliers and attending to three customers at once.

The Waasz AI Paradigm: We built an end-to-end operational back office directly inside WhatsApp. It gives African merchants an autonomous financial operating system that actively defends gross margins, recovers customer credit, blocks employee shrinkage, and translates daily trade into verifiable institutional creditworthiness.

Whether you drop a 5-second voice note in Nigerian Pidgin, snap a photo of a handwritten ledger page, or have your sales attendant record a counter sale, Waasz structures the ledger in the background with zero accounting jargon and zero behavioral friction.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about operating Waasz AI in your store, managing staff, and securing institutional growth loans.

How does Waasz AI prevent my shop attendants ("sales boys/girls") from stealing or seeing my profits?
Waasz AI features built-in Staff Role Permissions. You can link your sales attendants' WhatsApp numbers to your store with restricted staff access. They can log daily customer sales and issue digital receipts, but they have zero visibility into your wholesale cost prices, profit margins, or overall store cash balances.
How does Waasz AI recover overdue customer debts?
Whenever you make a credit sale, Waasz AI generates a digital IOU with the customer's phone number, agreed payment date, and items purchased. When the due date arrives, Waasz autonomously sends polite, automated WhatsApp reminder messages directly to the debtor with your verified bank account details, eliminating awkward manual confrontations.
Can I speak to Waasz AI in Nigerian Pidgin or send voice notes?
Yes. Waasz AI is trained on local African trading dialects. You can send a 5-second voice note in Nigerian Pidgin or English (e.g., "I sell 3 cartons of Indomie for 24,000 cash and 1 bag of rice on credit to Mama Ngozi"), and the engine parses every item, amount, and payment status accurately.
How does Waasz AI help me get bank loans or government grants without collateral?
By consistently logging your daily sales and expenses on WhatsApp, Waasz AI automatically builds your algorithmic Credit Score (300–850) and compiles a Stamped 2-Page Financial Health Profile PDF. Commercial banks, microfinance institutions, and SME grant bodies (such as SMEDAN) accept this stamped report as verifiable proof of business turnover.
Do I need to download a mobile app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store?
No. Waasz AI operates 100% inside WhatsApp. There are zero apps to download, zero passwords to remember, and zero phone storage consumed. If you know how to send a WhatsApp chat, you already know how to use Waasz AI.
What happens if my phone gets lost or stolen?
All your financial records, customer debt ledgers, and credit scores are encrypted and backed up securely in the cloud. As soon as you retrieve your phone number or log in on a new device, your entire business history is instantly accessible.
