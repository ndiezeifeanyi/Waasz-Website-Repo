The Core Reality

Business owners don't skip bookkeeping out of laziness.

Traditional bookkeeping software fails because it forces you to stop what you are doing, open a separate app, log into a dashboard, and fill out endless form fields. In the middle of serving three customers in a bustling shop, that simply never happens.

Waasz eliminates behavioral friction completely. Whether you type a short message, drop a 5-second voice note in Pidgin, or simply snap a photo of your handwritten sales book or POS slip at the end of the day, your books, debt balances, and inventory stay 100% accurate in real time.

Comprehensive Functional Catalog

Engineered for Every Aspect of SME Operations

Everything Waasz can do inside your WhatsApp thread — text, voice, image understanding, and financial analytics.

01 Bookkeeping

Daily Sales & Expense Bookkeeping

  • Multimodal Logging (Text, Voice, Photo): Text, speak in English/Pidgin, or snap photos of handwritten notebooks and POS printouts.
  • Multi-Item & Payment Parsing: Automatically splits complex sales and records Cash, Bank Transfer, POS, or Credit.
  • Conversational Corrections: Make instant edits (e.g. "Change last fuel expense to ₦4,000").
  • Audit Trail & Soft Deletions: Preserves an immutable log of changes for accountant-grade reliability.
02 Inventory

Inventory & Stock Management

  • Waybill & Stock Photo Scanning: Snap supplier waybills or delivery invoices to record restocks and unit costs in one step.
  • Automated Stock Deduction: Decrements quantities automatically whenever a sale is logged.
  • Low-Stock Proactive Alerts: Alerts you before critical goods run out of stock.
  • Spoilage & Write-offs: Record returns, shrinkage, or damaged goods with reason notes.
03 Debtors

Debt Tracking & Credit Management

  • Debtor Balances on Demand: Instantly check who owes you money and total outstanding sums.
  • Partial & Full Settlements: Easily log customer repayments over time.
  • Privacy-Safe Reminders: Drafts courteous payment reminders for you to forward. Waasz never contacts your customers directly.
04 Invoicing

Professional PDF Receipts & Invoices

  • Instant PDF Generation: Creates print-ready, professional PDF receipts on the spot.
  • Direct WhatsApp Delivery: Delivers the `.pdf` file right inside your chat to forward immediately.
  • Persistent Store Branding: Remembers your Business Name, CAC/tax info, address, and custom footers.
05 Analytics

Business Intelligence & Margin Analytics

  • True Gross Margin Reports: Calculates exact profit margins per product using real unit costs.

  • Pricing Simulations: Simulate discount scenarios and see required sales volume.
