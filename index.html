Frequently Asked Questions

How does Waasz AI prevent my shop attendants ("sales boys/girls") from stealing or seeing my profits?
Waasz AI features built-in Staff Role Permissions. You can link your sales attendants' WhatsApp numbers to your store with restricted staff access. They can log daily customer sales and issue digital receipts, but they have zero visibility into your wholesale cost prices, profit margins, or overall store cash balances.
How does Waasz AI recover overdue customer debts?
Whenever you make a credit sale, Waasz AI generates a digital IOU with the customer's phone number, agreed payment date, and items purchased. When the due date arrives, Waasz automatically sends polite WhatsApp reminder messages directly to the debtor with your verified bank account details, eliminating awkward manual confrontations.
Can I speak to Waasz AI in Nigerian Pidgin or send voice notes?
Yes. Waasz AI understands local African trading dialects and measurement units. How does Waasz AI help small businesses prepare for loans?
Most retail merchants get rejected for financing simply because they cannot prove their daily cash flow. Waasz AI turns your informal WhatsApp sales and expense chats into structured financial statements and a 300 to 850 credit score. Whenever you want to apply for a bank loan, microfinance credit, or SME grant, you can download your 2-page Financial Health Profile PDF as clear proof of your business turnover.
Do I need to download a mobile app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store?
No. Waasz AI operates 100% inside WhatsApp. There are zero apps to download, zero passwords to remember, and zero phone storage consumed. If you know how to send a WhatsApp chat, you already know how to use Waasz AI.
What happens if my phone gets lost or stolen?
All your financial records, customer debt ledgers, and credit scores are encrypted and backed up securely in the cloud and can be synced to your personal Google Drive. As soon as you retrieve your phone number or log in on a new device, your entire business history is immediately accessible.
